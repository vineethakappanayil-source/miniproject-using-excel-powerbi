# 📊 Retail Sales Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze retail sales performance, product and category performance, customer and time-based trends, payment methods, and sales details.

The dashboard transforms retail transaction data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPIs, slicers, page navigation, and drill-through analysis.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall retail sales performance
- Track total sales, transactions, and quantity
- Identify the highest-performing product categories
- Analyze sales by payment method
- Compare online and in-store sales
- Analyze sales trends by year and month
- Understand customer-level sales performance
- Provide detailed transaction-level analysis
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations

---

### 5️⃣ 💡 Insights & Recommendations

#### 🔍 Key Insights

- Butchers is the highest-performing category in terms of total sales.
- Cash, Digital Wallet, and Credit Card are the major payment methods.
- In-store sales are slightly higher than online sales.
- Total sales are approximately **$1.64M**, with around **70K units sold**.
- Sales performance varies across categories, with Butchers leading the other categories.

#### 📌 Recommendations

- Maintain sufficient stock for the Butchers category.
- Focus promotions on lower-performing categories to improve sales.
- Encourage customers to use preferred payment methods.
- Compare online and in-store performance regularly.
- Monitor category and product performance to identify growth opportunities.
